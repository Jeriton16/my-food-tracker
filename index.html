🌈 7-day rainbow meal ideas

Use these as mix-and-match ideas. Add the portions you actually eat in your diary. Choose water, unsweetened drinks and modest amounts of oil; vary vegetables and fruit through the week.

Day 1 — Breakfast: oats, milk, banana and chia. Lunch: rice, Goan fish curry, spinach and tomato. Snack: guava and a few almonds. Dinner: chapati, moong dal, carrot and cucumber salad.

Day 2 — Breakfast: 2 eggs, whole-wheat toast and orange. Lunch: chicken curry, rice, beetroot and cabbage. Snack: plain curd and papaya. Dinner: ragi bhakri, chickpea curry and green beans.

Day 3 — Breakfast: idli with sambar. Lunch: sardines, rice, pumpkin and purple cabbage. Snack: apple and walnuts. Dinner: tofu or paneer bhurji, chapati and tomato-cucumber salad.

Day 4 — Breakfast: vegetable omelette and toast. Lunch: brown rice, dal, okra and carrot. Snack: banana and curd. Dinner: grilled chicken, sweet potato or regular potato, broccoli and capsicum.

Day 5 — Breakfast: ragi porridge, milk and papaya. Lunch: prawn or fish curry, rice, spinach and beetroot. Snack: roasted chana and orange. Dinner: mixed bean usal, chapati and cucumber salad.

Day 6 — Breakfast: oats with apple, flaxseed and curd. Lunch: chicken xacuti (moderate coconut/oil), rice and cabbage salad. Snack: guava and peanuts. Dinner: dal khichdi with pumpkin and green vegetables.

Day 7 — Breakfast: moong chilla with curd. Lunch: mackerel, rice, tomato and leafy greens. Snack: seasonal fruit and almonds. Dinner: tofu/paneer or egg curry, chapati and mixed vegetables.

Rainbow guide: red (tomato, beetroot), orange/yellow (carrot, pumpkin, papaya), green (spinach, broccoli, okra), blue/purple (purple cabbage, brinjal), white/brown (banana, onion, beans, whole grains). Rotate colours rather than chasing a perfect plate every day.
